HCT LSC 1503 – LPA 5

Reflection: Preparing for My Interview Assessment

Before you start

Your interview assessment is coming soon. To prepare, you worked through the interview apps, wrote and improved your scripts, practised with a partner, and had a mock interview with your teacher.

Now think about all of that preparation. Answer the questions below in complete sentences. Be honest about your experience — there are no right or wrong answers here. Aim for three to four sentences per question.

1 How did you write your scripts? Describe the steps you followed.

Think about: where you got your ideas, which apps or notes you used, how you organised each answer, and how many times you changed it.

0 words
First, I…. After that, I… To find ideas for my answers, I used… I changed my script… times because…
2 Did you work with a partner while preparing? How did their feedback help you?

Think about: what they told you to change, what they said you did well, and what you did differently after their feedback.

0 words
I practised with… My partner told me that I should… After their feedback, I changed…
3 What did you learn from your mock interview with your teacher? What feedback did you get?

Think about: what went well, what surprised you, and the advice your teacher gave you.

0 words
In my mock interview, I did well when… My teacher advised me to… I was surprised that…
4 Which interview question are you most confident about, and which one still worries you? Explain why.

Think about: “Tell me about yourself,” your study skills, describing yourself, your experiences, and your future plans.

0 words
I feel most confident about… because… The question that still worries me is… This question is difficult for me because…
5 What have you learned about yourself as a speaker during this unit?

Think about: your confidence, your body language, your preparation habits, and how you deal with nerves.

0 words
I have learned that I am… When I feel nervous, I… At the start of this unit I…, but now I…
6 What will you do in the days before your interview to be fully ready?

Think about: what you will practise, how many times, what you will bring, and how you will prepare on the morning of the exam.

0 words
Before my interview, I will practise… I will bring… with me. On the morning of the exam, I will…
